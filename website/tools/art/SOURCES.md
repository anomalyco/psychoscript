`site/art/boss.jpg` and the avatar on `comets.svg` use Dax Raad’s actual portrait on [his Is Serverless Ready? speaker page](https://isserverlessready.com/speakers/thdxr.html), [source PNG](https://isserverlessready.com/assets/dax.3dab10fd.png). The speaker photo is saved as `design/dax-speaker.png`. The contour cut and copper/purple print separation are in `tools/art/boss-art.py`. The card text is fictional.

`design/dax-prefix.svg` is hand lettering; the six letters after it come from `design/lettering.png` at original resolution. Run `tools/art/boss-art.py` from repo root, then `bun tools/trace-takeover.mjs design/daxscript.png dax 9 3` for the two fields.

`site/art/fire.png` is packed by `tools/art/fire-atlas.mjs` from two flipbooks in Unity Labs' "Free VFX Image Sequences & Flipbooks" (Thomas Iché, Unity Technologies, 2016), released under CC0: the flame is `SmallFlame01-mini_16x4.tga` from [SmallFlame01-mini-flipbooks.zip](https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/SmallFlame01-mini-temperature/SmallFlame01-mini-flipbooks.zip) and the smoke is `CandleSmoke01_20x4.tga` from [CandleSmoke01-flipbooks.zip](https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/CandleSmoke01/CandleSmoke01-flipbooks.zip), both listed on [the release post](https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks) ("Here are some image sequences we want to share with you under CC0 license"). Unzip them and run `bun tools/art/fire-atlas.mjs SmallFlame01-mini_16x4.tga CandleSmoke01_20x4.tga site/art/fire.png`.
